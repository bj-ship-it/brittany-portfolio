Bio

I write emotionally explosive, unputdownable romance packed with spice, grit, and a whole lot of heart. From dark suspense, to contemporary, to sports romance, my stories are full of strong women, complicated men who would burn the world for their women, and tension that crackles off the page. I'm a sucker for a good plot twist, and a firm believer that love should feel like fire—whether it’s in fiction or in real life. If you’re into fierce heroines, emotionally wrecked men, and stories that will absolutely ruin your sleep schedule... welcome. You’re my kind of reader.
